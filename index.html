How to change your website

Signing in to edit
Your website is made with Google Sites. Go to sites.google.com (or press Edit my website above) and sign in with your Google account:
Username (email):
Password:
Once you are in, you can change the writing, photos, fonts, colours, layout and pages, as below.
  • Change any writing: in the editor, click the text and type.
  • Add a photo: on the right, Insert, then Images. Drag it where you want it.
  • Move things around: drag a section by the dotted handle on its left.
  • Fonts and colours: on the right, Themes.
  • Add a page (Showreel, Notes): on the right, Pages, then the + button.
  • Make it live: click Publish at the top right. Nothing changes for visitors until you do.
  • Go back to an earlier version: click the clock icon at the top of the editor. Every version is listed with its date and time. Click one, then Restore, then Publish. Your first version is named "Original, 13 Nov 2026".
  • The editor opens in the Google account that runs your website. If it asks you to sign in, use that account (the same one this page sends emails from).

    Let the job sites email you

    The daily search cannot sign in to LinkedIn, Guardian Jobs or other sites, and you should never give anyone your passwords. Instead, set up free email alerts on the sites below yourself. Send them to your application inbox (the Gmail account this page sends from). They then appear in Correspondence, under Job alerts, where you can read them in full and flick through.

    1. LinkedIn: search Jobs for, say, "producer" with the Remote and Part time filters, then switch on "Set alert". Make sure your LinkedIn email is your application inbox, or add it under Settings, Sign in and security, Email addresses.
    2. Guardian Jobs: make a free account with your application inbox, search, then "Create job alert".
    3. CharityJob, Timewise, Arts Jobs, Culture Jobs, Escape the City, Indeed: the same: search with the remote or part time filter, then save the search as an email alert.
    4. BBC Careers and France Médias Monde: sign up for their job alerts if you would like to hear about BBC or RFI and France 24 roles.

    Set a saved search or email alert on each of these. Suggested searches: "producer remote part time", "head of communications remote", "editorial consultant", "chargé de communication télétravail".
